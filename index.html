About

MARCI Daily Global Maps

Mars Daily Global Maps (MDGMs) are built from Mars Reconnaissance Orbiter Mars Color Imager (MARCI) wide-angle swaths. Each one combines 13 consecutive global swaths covering about one sol, on a simple cylindrical grid (90°S–90°N, 180°W–180°E), imaged at about 3 pm local time.

Data

  • Now: Wang et al., MRO MARCI MDGMs Version 2, 0.1° color maps (Harvard Dataverse; full archive at the PDS Imaging Node Annex). Tiled to WebP; black no-data is transparent.
  • Next: full-resolution MDGMs processed from MARCI EDRs with ISIS.
  • Basemaps: Viking MDIM 2.1 colorized mosaic and MGS MOLA color shaded relief, via NASA Mars Trek WMTS.

Mars calendar

Solar longitude (Ls) and Mars Sol Date follow Allison & McEwen (2000), the Mars24 algorithm. Mars Year numbering follows Clancy et al. (2000), with MY 1 beginning 1955-04-11. The striped band on the timeline marks the dust storm season (Ls 180°–360°).

Coordinates

Planetocentric latitude, east-positive longitude. Distances use a sphere of radius 3396.19 km.
