MARCI Daily Global Maps

This viewer shows Mars Daily Global Maps (MDGMs) built from Mars Reconnaissance Orbiter Mars Color Imager (MARCI) wide-angle swaths. Each map is composed from 13 consecutive global swaths covering roughly one sol, on a simple cylindrical grid (90°S–90°N, 180°W–180°E).

Data

  • Phase 1: Wang, H. et al., MRO MARCI MDGMs Version 2, 0.1° color JPEGs — Harvard Dataverse doi:10.7910/DVN/U3766S; full archive at the PDS Imaging Node Annex.
  • Phase 2: Full-resolution MDGMs processed from MARCI EDRs with ISIS (marci2isis → spiceinit → marcical → photomet → cam2map → mosaic), served as tiles.
  • Basemaps: Viking MDIM 2.1 colorized mosaic and MGS MOLA color shaded relief, via NASA Mars Trek WMTS.

Mars calendar

Solar longitude (Ls) and Mars Sol Date follow Allison & McEwen (2000), the Mars24 algorithm. Mars Year numbering follows Clancy et al. (2000), where MY 1 begins 1955-04-11. The shaded band on the timeline marks the dust storm season (Ls 180°–360°).

Coordinates

Planetocentric latitude and east-positive longitude. Distances use a sphere of radius 3396.19 km.
